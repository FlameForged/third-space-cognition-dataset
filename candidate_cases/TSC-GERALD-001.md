# Candidate Case: TSC-GERALD-001

**Status:** Candidate for future coding; not part of the frozen 27-observation manuscript snapshot and not yet incorporated into `HI_Data.csv`.

**Date:** 2026-08-10

## Why preserve this case

This interaction is not being treated as evidence of emergent AI behavior. The model did not claim to be “Gerald,” adopt a new identity, or display unexplained autonomous behavior.

The case is potentially useful as an example of **interactionally co-constructed conversational artifacts and shared semantic shorthand** during sustained human-AI interaction.

## Core sequence

1. A serious discussion about AI ontology and anthropomorphism shifted to the homunculus fallacy.
2. The AI spontaneously named a hypothetical internal digital homunculus **“Gerald.”**
3. The user immediately adopted and elaborated the character.
4. The AI then incorporated the user’s elaborations, and “Gerald” rapidly became mutually intelligible shorthand for an unnecessary human-like observer implicitly inserted into explanations of artificial cognition.
5. Later, the AI wrote **“impressive emergent outputs.”** The user visually misread **“outputs”** as **“octopus.”**
6. The user corrected the reading but the phrase **“impressive emergent octopus”** was subsequently incorporated into the shared interaction vocabulary.
7. The interaction then became explicitly metacognitive: both participants discussed artifact provenance, reciprocal elaboration, humor, distraction, lexical retrieval interference, and the affective value of the exchange.

## Provenance distinction

### Gerald
- Initial name/artifact: AI-generated.
- Uptake: user-generated.
- Subsequent elaboration and stabilized meaning: reciprocal.

### “Impressive emergent octopus”
- Trigger phrase (“impressive emergent outputs”): AI-generated.
- Perceptual substitution (“octopus”): user-generated reading error.
- Subsequent elaboration and stabilized meaning: reciprocal.

This distinction is methodologically useful: **interactional co-construction does not require erasing turn-level provenance.** An artifact may have an identifiable initial source while acquiring a shared function through reciprocal interaction.

## Important exclusion

Do **not** code this case as “AI emergent behavior” merely because the resulting shared vocabulary was unplanned or surprising. The more precise candidate category is **interaction-level co-construction / shared representational artifact formation**.

## Potential research question

How do sustained human-AI interactions generate locally meaningful shorthand, metaphors, or conceptual artifacts that become increasingly compressed and mutually intelligible over time, and how can provenance be preserved while analyzing the jointly developed function of those artifacts?
